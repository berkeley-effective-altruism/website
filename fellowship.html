Fellowship Program

The EA intro fellowship consists of 5-7 person discussion groups for interested students! Groups will meet weekly for two hours: one hour of reading and one hour of discussion.

How it works

Format

You'll meet every week with your group of 5-7 students for six weeks, and do all of the reading together!

Curriculum

We'll discuss how we prioritize what to work on, what issues EAs work on currently, and what the best ways are to make an impact.

Commitment

2 hours per week, 6 weeks

Who should apply

Anyone who is interested in doing good better, no prior experience required :)
